Official policy research

Federal Student Aid describes Pell awards using enrollment intensity and school-defined full-time loads; moving from 12 to 9 credits can change an award to 75% intensity. This is not a universal rule that every aid program is lost below 12 credits. 2026–27 FSA Handbook: Pell formulas.

The limited federal-loan transition exception involves enrollment in the program before the cutoff, a prior Direct Loan for that program, and continued enrollment. A start date alone cannot establish the exception. The institution must confirm the student’s program, history and applicable limits. Federal Student Aid: transition exception, School-determined requirements.
