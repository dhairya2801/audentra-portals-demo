Implementation audit · October 8, 2026

Student portal: what shipped locally, and what remains.

Ajlan’s feedback and Dr Zaibis’s contributions across four meetings and all eight supplied PDFs. First-time onboarding is excluded. This audit covers the meeting-driven UI work and follow-up fixes, not all pre-existing changes in the checkpoint commit.

The answer

The remaining in-scope UI feedback is now implemented or available as a clearly labeled interactive preview. Points are restored, service discovery is expanded, financial assumptions have detailed controls, offer scenarios are connected, and payment lifecycle states are reviewable. This does not mean every meeting action is complete: university policy/data corrections, unsupported backend integrations, research sessions and human sign-off remain explicitly open below.

  • Enrollment: restored points and bookstore value, daily incentive example, dependency-aware sorting, real review reasons, month calendar, replayable guide and sample transcript.
  • Appointments: compact service rows, assigned people/teams/offices, six additional service previews, conditional international services, centered date/time picker and safe meeting links; existing canonical bookings retained.
  • Financial Overview: potential-offer/expiry chart states, interactive tuition math, scholarship renewal examples, published housing/meal context, daily/term-aware budget units, synchronized work-study and a loan-specific remainder.
  • Aid and Loans: connected accept/decline/partial-loan previews, all-offers scenario, full disbursement details and an official-source federal loan guide.
  • Payments: accepted/posted breakdowns, active-plan/paid/credit/refund-received states, two payment choices and one agreement-specific schedule.
  • Expenses: aligned tables, one shared Housing & meals header, and coverage shown only here.
  • Shared: original gradient headers restored, duplicate Financials navigation removed, and Review offer now opens the named offer directly.

How to read the audit

“Included” means the stated UI behavior exists; individual rows identify retained functionality and mock assumptions. “UI preview” means a clickable flow without canonical submission. “Partial” identifies a material gap. “Not included” records an outstanding request or research step. “Excluded” records a deliberate scope boundary or meeting deferral. Counts describe the consolidated rows, not a percentage-complete score.
